My Way to Ace PSM I Exam

Study for professional scrum master I exam in a efficient way

Recently I passed my PSM I exam with 90% score within 10 days. And I would like to share some tips on how to do it efficiently.

TL;DR

Here is the list of materials I used during my studying for PSM I:

  1. Scrum Open (classmarker.com) [A mock up test for PSM I]
  2. Scrum Guide | Scrum Guides [The core reading for scrum]
  3. Scrum Glossary | Scrum.org [Quick reference for scrum terminology]
  4. Suggested Reading for PSM I (blogs and articles about scrum practice)
  5. PSM I Prep Quiz ( An unofficial scrum mock test based on 2017 scrum guide)

How to use these materials

  • I read the scrum guide for 3 times.
  • I read scrum glossary and scrum blogs to further enhance my understanding.
  • I tested my understanding of scrum with PSM I Prep Quiz 2 times. And tried to understand why the answer is correct.
  • I did the scrum open mock up exam till I got 100% correct.

Overall, for the purpose of exam, I think the scrum open mock up exam is the key to success. Because plenty of exam questions are directly from the mock up exam database.

Hope this can help you prepare your scrum exam!
