# Health Intelligence Shiny App

A comprehensive hospital recommendation system designed to help users find the best hospital for their specific medical conditions. This interactive tool allows patients to make informed decisions about their healthcare options based on various metrics and performance indicators.

## Features

The Health Intelligence application provides:

- Personalized hospital recommendations based on user-specified conditions
- Geographic visualization of hospital locations
- Comparative analysis of hospital performance metrics
- Quality of care indicators and patient satisfaction scores
- Treatment cost comparisons across different facilities


## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: R Shiny for interactive web application
- **Backend**: R for data processing and statistical analysis
- **Data Sources**: Medicare Hospital Compare datasets, patient satisfaction surveys, and treatment cost databases
- **Visualization**: Leaflet for interactive maps, ggplot2 for statistical visualizations

## Project Goals

The primary goal of this project is to democratize access to hospital quality information, empowering patients to make data-driven decisions about their healthcare. By providing a user-friendly interface to complex healthcare metrics, patients can find facilities that best meet their specific needs, potentially leading to better treatment outcomes and patient satisfaction.

## Try It Yourself

The Hospital Intelligence application is deployed and accessible online. You can explore the tool and find hospital recommendations based on your own conditions by visiting the [live application](https://mingzhong.shinyapps.io/hospital_intelligence/).
