# H1B Visa Application Dashboard

An interactive data visualization tool designed to help international students make informed decisions about job applications by providing insights into industries and companies with high H-1B visa approval rates.

## Project Overview

The H1B Dashboard aggregates and analyzes H-1B visa application data to identify trends, patterns, and success rates across different dimensions including:

- Industries with highest approval rates
- Companies most likely to sponsor work visas
- Geographic distribution of H-1B approvals
- Salary ranges for different positions and locations
- Evolution of approval rates over time

## Data Sources and Methodology

The dashboard utilizes public data from the U.S. Department of Labor's Office of Foreign Labor Certification (OFLC), which includes:
- Employer application details
- Job titles and positions
- Proposed salaries
- Work locations
- Application status (certified, denied, withdrawn)

The raw data undergoes extensive cleaning and transformation to enable meaningful analysis and visualization.

## Impact and Utility

International students face significant uncertainty when pursuing employment opportunities in the United States. This tool addresses this challenge by:

1. Providing data-driven insights about companies with strong track records of visa sponsorship
2. Highlighting industries with higher approval rates
3. Identifying optimal geographic locations for specific job roles
4. Offering salary benchmarks to inform negotiation strategies

## Future Developments

Planned enhancements include:
- Predictive modeling to forecast approval probabilities
- Integration of additional visa categories (L-1, O-1, etc.)
- Personalized recommendation engine based on user profiles
- Time-series analysis of policy impacts on approval rates

## Access the Dashboard

The H1B Dashboard is publicly available online. You can explore the interactive tool at [H1B Visualization](https://mingzhong.shinyapps.io/H1BViz/).
